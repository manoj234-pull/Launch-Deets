# Bravo countdown hold

## Preflight observation
During the Launch-Deets Bravo countdown on 3 May 2026, valve actuator VL-02 showed a 0.8 degree position oscillation. Its component record is timed at 09:12:00 UTC. VL-02 is also called the oxidizer feed valve actuator in this fictional archive (REC-L7-006; DOC-L7-004).

## Countdown and launch
At 09:28:44 UTC, the VL-02 position oscillation triggered a 17 second hold. Countdown resumed after readings stabilized, and the launch proceeded. This incident was classified moderate and closed (REC-L7-007). Bravo lifted off from Pad B at 09:30 UTC (REC-L7-005). The oscillation magnitude is measured in degrees; the hold duration is measured in seconds.

## Later corrective action
On 6 May 2026, technicians replaced the actuator bearing and added a countdown vibration check. The action record was verified on that date (REC-L7-008; DOC-L7-006). This maintenance action followed the 3 May launch; it was not the same event as the stable readings that allowed the countdown to resume.
