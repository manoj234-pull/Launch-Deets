# Echo bus B voltage transient

## Preflight and launch
On 2 August 2026 at 12:51 UTC, power distribution unit PD-11 passed its load test at 99.1 percent before the Launch-Deets Echo launch (REC-L7-018). Echo lifted off from Pad B at 13:15 UTC (REC-L7-017; DOC-L7-013).

## Incident observation
After stage separation, bus B on PD-11 registered a 33 millisecond voltage sag. The incident record is timed at 13:18:20 UTC and identifies this event as INC-L7-05. It was classified major and closed after analysis (REC-L7-019). The 33 millisecond value is the duration of the sag, not a percentage or a voltage magnitude.

## Later corrective action
The archive links INC-L7-05 to corrective action ACT-L7-05. On 5 August 2026 at 15:45 UTC, the team increased bus B capacitor bank capacity by 8 percent; the modification was verified on 5 August (REC-L7-020; DOC-L7-015). The action followed the 2 August launch. The 8 percent increase describes the modification and is distinct from the earlier 99.1 percent load-test measurement.
